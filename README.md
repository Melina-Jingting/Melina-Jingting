### 🧠 Current Focus & Research
*   **Neural Decoding:** Foundation models have found success in text, audio, visual data by finding shared representations across large data corpora. Can this impact be translated to neural decoders for efficient transfer between subjects and tasks? Inspired by the inference efficiency of State Space Models (SSMs), we found that they can be much faster to train; that they can match (even surpass) Transformers-based models >10x bigger! This was achieved by providing a strong inductive bias, grounded in control theory. We've also taken the opportunity to interpret the neural dynamics that are shared across subjects. This work, capturing shared dynamical representations, was developed at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit and won Cisco's Prize for Best Thesis Project at UCL.
