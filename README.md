### 🧠 Current Focus & Research
*   **Neural Decoding:** I am architecting agentic AI workflows orchestrating experiments across distributed HPC clusters to improve the interpretability of foundation models for BCIs. We found that lightweight State Space Models (SSMs) are much faster to train and can match (even surpass) transformers-based models greater than 10x their size, by providing a strong inductive bias! We've also taked this as an opportunity to interpret the neural dynamics that are shared across subjects. This work, capturing shared dynamical representations, was developed at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit and won Cisco's Prize for Best Thesis Project at UCL.
