### 🧠 Current Focus & Research
*   **Neural Decoding:** I am architecting agentic AI workflows orchestrating experiments across distributed HPC clusters to improve the interpretability of foundation models for BCIs. My research demonstrates that lightweight State Space Models (SSMs) provide a strong inductive bias and can achieve state-of-the-art results, surpassing models greater than 10x their size. This work, capturing shared dynamical representations, was developed at the Gatsby Computational Neuroscience Unit and won Cisco's Prize for Best Thesis Project at UCL.
